WallCal

The goal of this project was to learn embedded systems, microcontroller programming, hardware prototyping, and 3D modeling by building an ESP32-powered e-ink wall calendar that syncs with my online calendar. Coming from a software background, most of the hardware side was new to me. Along the way I've learned to prototype circuits with breadboards and handle physical inputs like a rotary encoder to control the program's state. WallCal is still in progress.

Technologies:

  • C++
  • Arduino/ESP32
  • PlatformIO
  • SDL3
